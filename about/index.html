About Justin Erasmus

Justin Erasmus is an AI automation specialist and hybrid music producer based in Alberton, south of Johannesburg. He works on practical AI and business automation for South African organisations under the Business BrAIn name, and produces music through AlgoRythm Hybrid Music Production (AHMP), his human + AI production approach. His main recording project, Prismatic Wave, has released 37 records on Apple Music since February 2025.

Verifiable timeline

  • 11 Feb 2025: first Prismatic Wave album, Echo Of The Streets (12 tracks), released (℗ 2025 Justin Erasmus). Listen on Spotify.
  • Feb 2025: nine singles follow in the same month.
  • Oct 2025: releases start carrying the ℗ line “AlgoRythm AI Music Production”.
  • 30 Oct 2025: named as co-founder of ZeekQuence AI in a paid advertorial in the Alberton Record (see Media).
  • Dec 2025 onward: releases carry the ℗ line “AlgoRythm Hybrid Music Production”.
  • 14 Mar 2026: latest release on Apple Music at time of checking, Ghost Choir In My Skull. Total: 37 releases, 152 tracks.
