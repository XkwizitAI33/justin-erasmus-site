Hybrid music producer, Alberton

The story in short

Justin Erasmus is a hybrid music producer from Alberton, Gauteng, and the founder of AlgoRythm Hybrid Music Production (founded 2023). He created the hybrid artist Prismatic Wave, behind the 12-track hip-hop album Echo Of The Streets, dated 11 February 2025 on Spotify and Apple Music. He writes his own lyrics and treats AI as a tool, not the artist.

His central argument is that every tool that changed music, from the TR-808 drum machine to the synthesizer, the sampler and the laptop studio, was first written off as fake or as cheating, and then reshaped the sound of the world. He believes AI is on the same road, and he wants a human hand on the wheel. His three-word thesis is evolve or dissolve.

Justin says Echo Of The Streets was South Africa's first AI album. That is his claim. TechCentral reports that Spotify and Apple Music date it to 11 February 2025, “more than seven months before” a later 2025 album widely reported as the country's first AI album.

Why human-led hybrid music isn't “slop”

“AI slop” describes content where nobody is steering. Justin's process is the AlgoRythm 4-Stage Hybrid Music Framework, with a human at both ends:

  1. 01

    Concept

    Human vision + AI exploration

  2. 02

    Generation

    AI-assisted creation

  3. 03

    Human Curation

    Producer intervention

  4. 04

    Production & Delivery

    Final hybrid master

Human → AI → Human → Audience
AI creates possibilities. Human creativity gives them identity.

  • Not one prompt and one click. A human sets the concept, AI assists with generation, and a human curates, produces and delivers the final master.
  • The AI is not the artist. Justin writes his own lyrics. Prismatic Wave is a hybrid artist identity he created and directs.
  • Worth asking about. By his own count he has released 11 albums and 5 EPs in 13 months, plus 20 singles. Where do the human decisions sit at that pace?

He is a member of SAMRO and SAMPRA. Membership is separate from registration of individual works and is not an approval of any creation process. The method is written up in his 2025 white paper, “The AlgoRythm Framework”.
