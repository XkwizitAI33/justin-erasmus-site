When AI wrote the AI policy: lessons for every South African business

By Justin Erasmus · 2026-10-13

#ai-policy#responsible-ai#small-business

South Africa's draft national AI policy was withdrawn after it was found to contain AI-generated errors, and a new draft is expected by March, according to Daily Maverick (21 September 2026). The Sunday Times reported on 3 October 2026 that the policy is getting a second chance.

Why this matters for a small business

If a national policy team can publish unchecked AI output, so can a five-person company: a quote with an invented clause, a client email that cites a regulation that doesn't exist, a marketing claim nobody verified.

A simple checking routine

  1. Treat AI output as a first draft. A person signs off on anything that goes to a client, regulator or the public.
  2. Check every citation. Open each link and confirm it says what the draft claims.
  3. Keep a source list. Record where facts came from.
  4. Be careful with personal information. The Information Regulator is paying close attention to AI under POPIA (ITWeb).

The takeaway
