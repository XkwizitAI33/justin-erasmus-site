What I learned releasing 37 human + AI records in 13 months

By Justin Erasmus · 2026-10-14

#ai-music#hybrid-production#prismatic-wave

Between 11 February 2025 and 14 March 2026, Prismatic Wave released 37 records on Apple Music: albums, EPs and singles adding up to 152 tracks. Every one was made with my hybrid approach, AlgoRythm Hybrid Music Production: I set the direction and make the creative decisions, and AI tools help generate and iterate.

The numbers, honestly

Output is not audience. At the time of writing, Prismatic Wave has 9 monthly listeners on Spotify. Releasing a lot of music is the easy part. Getting it heard is a different job.

Two bursts, not a steady stream

The catalogue came in two waves: ten releases in February 2025, then a long run from late October 2025 to March 2026 when the label line moved to AlgoRythm AI Music Production and then AlgoRythm Hybrid Music Production.
