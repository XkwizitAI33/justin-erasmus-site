AI & Business Automation

Through Business BrAIn I offer practical AI and automation for small and growing South African businesses: systems that do real work, with human oversight where it matters.

Offerings

AI Readiness Audit

A review of your processes, data and tools to find where AI and automation can realistically save time.

AI implementation & AI agents

Designing and setting up assistants and agents that handle defined tasks inside your existing tools.

Workflow automation

Connecting the systems you already use so repetitive admin runs automatically.

WhatsApp AI customer service

AI-assisted responses on WhatsApp with handover to a person.

AI Fundamentals for Business & Work

Plain-language training for teams and non-technical staff on using AI tools safely and productively.

How I work

  1. Discover: understand the business and its goals.
  2. Audit: map processes and tools.
  3. Identify opportunities: pick the low-risk, high-value jobs first.
  4. Design and build the solution.
  5. Integrate, train and improve.

Responsible AI

Solutions are designed with POPIA in mind, keep a human in the loop for consequential decisions, and are transparent with customers about when they are talking to AI.
