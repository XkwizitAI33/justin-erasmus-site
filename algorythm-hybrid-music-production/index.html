AlgoRythm Hybrid Music Production

Where Human Creativity Meets Artificial Intelligence. AlgoRythm Hybrid Music Production (AHMP) was founded in 2023 by producer Justin Erasmus. It combines AI with human creative direction to produce music, beats, jingles, soundtracks and commercial audio, and is the home of the hybrid artist Prismatic Wave.

Human → AI → Human → Audience
AI creates possibilities. Human creativity gives them identity.

Justin sets out the method in a self-published white paper, “The AlgoRythm Framework: A White Paper on the Hybrid Music Production Process” (2025, Academia.edu). As TechCentral summarises it, he writes the raw lyrics himself, uses AI to analyse structure, rhyme and syllables, then rewrites everything; a single song typically needs eight to 15 instrumental versions.
