AI in Music & Hybrid Production

AlgoRythm Hybrid Music Production (AHMP) is my approach to making music with AI: people set the direction, write and choose; AI tools generate and help iterate; the final selection, sequencing and release decisions stay human.

Prismatic Wave

Prismatic Wave is the main AHMP project: 37 releases (152 tracks) on Apple Music from 11 February 2025 to 14 March 2026, spanning albums, EPs and singles.
