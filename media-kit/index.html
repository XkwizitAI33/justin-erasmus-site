Long bio (150 words)

Justin Erasmus is an AI automation specialist and hybrid music producer based in Alberton, Gauteng, south of Johannesburg. Under the Business BrAIn name he offers practical AI and automation to South African businesses: AI readiness audits, AI agents, workflow automation, WhatsApp AI customer service and AI Fundamentals for Business & Work, a plain-language training offer for teams. His approach puts people in charge of decisions, with AI handling defined tasks and humans reviewing consequential output. In music, he developed AlgoRythm Hybrid Music Production (AHMP), a method in which he sets the creative direction, writes and selects, while AI tools such as Suno help generate and iterate. His main project, Prismatic Wave, released its first album, Echo Of The Streets, on 11 February 2025. By March 2026 the catalogue had grown to 37 releases and 152 tracks, available on Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer and Amazon Music.
